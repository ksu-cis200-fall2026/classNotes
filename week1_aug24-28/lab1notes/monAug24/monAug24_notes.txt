    -> exams (3 midterms) - 45%

- policies
    -> labs. everybody gets 4 drops.
        full credit: finished before the end 
            OR if you work the whole time (even if not finished)

    -> tutorials. 
        no drops (but talk to me if something comes up)
        can submit up to 3 days late for 10% off per day
        try to finish before Monday's lab

    -> projects.
        drop lowest grade 
        can submit up to 3 days late for 10% off per day

    -> exams
        if you do better on the final than one of the midterms
        the final grade replaces your lowest midterm 

        miss exam: can make it up before next class period 
            otherwise, final will replace it
