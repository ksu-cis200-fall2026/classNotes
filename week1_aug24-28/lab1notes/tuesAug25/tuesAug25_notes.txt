        will get full credit if you finish OR if you work the whole time

    - tutorials

    - projects
        about 11
        drop the lowest 
        submit up to 3 days late for 10% off per day 

    - exams (3 midterm exam, 1 final)
        if your final is better than one of your midterms,
        your final grade will replace the lowest midterm grade 

        if you miss an exam, you can make it up if you can arrange
            it with me before the next class period 
        otherwise, your final grade will replace it
